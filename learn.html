About Us

Pain itself is loved, pursued, and desired—not because it is pain, but because it is a source of great labor and suffering. To take a trivial example, who among us would undertake strenuous physical exercise if not to obtain some advantage from it? Furthermore, who would choose to be in pain—to seek it out and desire it—simply because it is pain, and not for the sake of some resulting benefit? Those who abandon their duties are at fault; they lack the strength of character—that is, the capacity for hard work—required to fulfill their obligations.
